Privacy Policy

Adaptive Training Planner (the "app"), by Samuel Cadby. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

The short version: the app works out your training plan on your own iPhone. I don't run any servers for it, I don't have accounts, and I can't see your data.

What the app uses

  • Apple Health. With your permission, the app reads workouts, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep to work out your training load and readiness. Release builds only read from Health; they never write to it.
  • Calendars. With your permission, the app writes your planned sessions to a calendar you choose, and reads that calendar to keep it in step. It doesn't read your other calendars' events for any other purpose.
  • Notifications. With your permission, the app sends local reminders and alerts. They are created on your device, not by a server.
  • What you enter. Your objectives, availability, settings, check-ins (how you feel, illness or injury) and workout ratings.

Where your data goes

Everything is processed and stored on your device. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services, and your data is never sent to me or to anyone else.

If you turn on iCloud sync, the app's own data is stored in your private iCloud (CloudKit) database so it can appear on your other devices. That data belongs to you and sits in your Apple account; I can't access it. It's covered by Apple's own privacy terms.

Where Apple Intelligence is available, any language-model features run on your device with Apple's on-device model.

Exports

The app can export your data or a diagnostic file through the iOS share sheet. This only happens when you ask for it, and the file goes wherever you choose to send it.

Apple's TestFlight and App Store

If you install the app through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect crash reports and usage information under its own policies. I don't receive your personal data from this. Apple's policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device. Data in your iCloud account is removed from Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage. You can withdraw Health, Calendar and Notification access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Health data

Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them (it collects no data at all).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
